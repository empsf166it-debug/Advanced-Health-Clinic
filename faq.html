    General Questions

    Our clinic is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We also have a 24/7 emergency department for urgent medical needs.

    While we do accept walk-ins for general consultations, we highly recommend booking an appointment to avoid long wait times. For specialist consultations, an appointment is mandatory.

    We accept most major insurance plans including MediCare Plus, Global Health Ins, and United Care. Please visit our Appointment page or contact our billing department to verify your specific coverage.

    You can access your medical records securely through our online patient portal. Once you create an account, you can view test results, past diagnoses, and upcoming appointments.

    Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of any medications you are currently taking, and any relevant medical records from previous healthcare providers.
